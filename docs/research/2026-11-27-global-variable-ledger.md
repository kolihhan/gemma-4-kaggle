## Next experiment, CPU-first and falsifiable
Pre-register a retrospective qualitative and executable audit of five stable failures **plus** a contrast sample of solved tasks and mixed outcomes (avoid conditioning only on failure). The five are a diagnosis set, never a promotion set. Review issue + original visible source first without reference patch or grade, freeze explicit requirements and observable expected behavior, then compare available traces and final patches. Grade correctness using existing official result; gold/no-op used only by evaluator after rubric frozen. Record evidence availability and reviewer disagreement, separate multi-label causal possibilities from verified causes. Where a legitimate visible witness is executable, measure whether it separates already-retained correct/incorrect patches with blocked cases and cost retained. No new GPU, submission, training, or modification of hidden grader. If relevant data are inaccessible, report unresolved instead of inventing labels.

Primary research question: does the solver lack necessary evidence, misunderstand adequate evidence, implement wrong behavior, or fail execution? At least two competing hypotheses with falsifiers before each intervention. For future prospective test, use disjoint fresh tasks, same model/harness/budget unless the budget itself is the treatment, real resolved count and paired rescues/regressions primary.
